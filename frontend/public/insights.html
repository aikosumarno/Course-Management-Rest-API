Room Type Distribution Across Campus

Number of rooms of each type across all buildings. Helps faculty and schedulers find buildings with the right room configuration, and identifies imbalances to guide future infrastructure planning.

Room Size Distribution by Building

Number of small (≤40 seats), medium (41–100 seats), and large (100+ seats) rooms per building. Helps course schedulers identify mismatches between room capacity and course enrollment needs, improving scheduling efficiency and space utilization.

Course Enrollment Trends Over Time

Track enrollment patterns across years for specific courses or departments. Helps administrators identify growing demand for courses, plan faculty hiring, allocate classroom resources, and inform strategic curriculum decisions based on student interest trends.

Tip: By default, shows the 10 courses with the highest average student enrollment across the years they were offered. Select a department to filter, or choose specific courses to compare trends.
